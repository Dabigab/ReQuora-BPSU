Tips for a great report

  • Name the brand, colour and size.
  • Mention marks only the owner would know, such as stickers, scratches or initials.
  • Add a clear photo if you have one.
  • For found items, say where you left it (for example, handed to the main gate guard).

Your privacy

Your full name and contact details are never shown to the public. The Lost & Found Office verifies every claim in person.
